Record lifecycle
Nothing is ever deleted. Every step is an envelope write, logged with who decided it.
Arrival
Bound, matched or new. A held arrival waits in review, and nothing is minted.
Active
Attached (product facts fold down) or standing alone (holds everything). Attach and detach any time.
Archived
Reason: sold, duplicate, mistake, merged… Folded values copied on. Left alone.
Pending instructions
From the parent: +key, −key, −key → other. Applied on touch, by drain or by hand.
Cascade
The package decides: archive the units too, or leave them.
