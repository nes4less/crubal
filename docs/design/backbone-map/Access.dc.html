Access: edges
Every access fact is an edge: who, relation, mode, on which record, granted by whom. Records nest by within along the top; people and systems hold edges from below and the side. The sports facility is an example: any nesting of any kinds works the same way.

You grant only what you hold, and an edge is only as good as its grantor: when the coordinator's edge ends on Jun 30, the captain's edge lapses with it. Every chain ends at the owner, who alone holds the key: erase, export, transfer, and grant access to other owners. A person can own their own collection, lists and notes. Membership is an edge, not containment, so a player can play at three facilities and none of them owns their record. Relations (mode none) never grant anything. Systems such as the enricher request menu items like app scopes and hold a control edge with exactly those.
